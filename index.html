Predictive Intent Coordination (PIC) Policy for Collision Avoidance in Autonomous Vehicles

Arizona State University, Mar 2025

Developed a Predictive Intent Coordination policy that forecasts neighbor vehicle maneuvers and preemptively overrides conflicting evasive decisions using Control Barrier Functions. MATLAB simulations show PIC reduces collisions by 80% while improving traffic throughput and ride comfort compared to reactive rule-based safety systems.

Arduino-Based PID Motor Position Control: Design and Comparative Analysis

Arizona State University, Sep 2024

Implemented and compared P, PD, and PID controllers for Arduino-based DC motor position tracking using hardware-in-the-loop MATLAB/Simulink experiments. Results validate that full PID control achieves superior performance with <2% overshoot overshoot and <1 radian steady-state error when tracking sinusoidal trajectories.
